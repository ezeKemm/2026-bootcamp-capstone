# Risk Register

Risks to delivering and defending the Northstar CRM slice, with mitigations, an owner and a due date for each (Lab 48 Exercise 5; capstone brief §6 "risk register with mitigations"). Under brief §8.4 rule 6, a security finding or deployment gap only costs full credit if it is **not** listed here as an accepted residual risk with an owner and a date, so gaps are recorded honestly rather than left out.

**Likelihood / impact:** Low · Medium · High. **Status:** Open (work still to do) · Mitigated (controls in place, keep watching) · Accepted (residual risk the team has agreed to carry). **Owner** is the role from [`team.md`](team.md).


| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Due date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Secret leak:** the JWT secret or database password is committed to the repo | Low | High | `.env` is git-ignored; `.env.example` holds placeholders only; `JWT_SECRET` has no default, so the app will not start without one; agreement 9 in `team.md` forbids secrets in commits | Technical Lead | 2026-10-11 | Mitigated |
| R2 | **Event lost during a Kafka outage:** the interaction is saved, but its event is never published (no outbox or re-send) | Medium | Medium | Event is published only after the database commit; publish fails within 10 s and is logged with interaction and correlation IDs (NFR-4). A transactional outbox is out of scope for the course | Technical Lead | 2026-10-11 | Accepted |
| R3 | **Kafka lag or failure during the live demo** delays or hides the event part of the story | Medium | Medium | Bounded consumer retries (3 × 1 s) and a dead-letter topic (NFR-3); start from a fresh `docker compose up -d`; rehearse the demo; keep test output as backup evidence | Presentation Lead | 2026-10-11 | Mitigated |
| R4 | **Incomplete JWT negative tests:** no test for an expired token or for a `403` on a wrong role | Medium | Medium | `SecurityPathTest` covers missing and invalid tokens (401). Add an expired-token test; add a 403 test once any endpoint is restricted to one role | Technical Lead | 2026-10-11 | Open |
| R5 | **Activate customer not built (CAP-18):** the backlog story and the profile's Activate button fail; 409 vs 422 for "already active" is undecided | High | Medium | Build the `activateCustomer` slice (domain rule, security rule and frontend button already exist); decide 409 vs 422 and update `openapi.yaml` | Technical Lead | 2026-10-11 | Open |
| R6 | **No deployment yet:** no Dockerfiles, image workflow, deployed smoke test or rollback, and the target changed from OpenShift to k3s on AWS | High | High | Record the target decision; build the image, deploy, smoke test (CAP-21) and rollback plan. If not finished, keep this row as an accepted residual risk with the date it will be done | Team Lead | 2026-10-11 | Open |
| R7 | **E2E tests only run locally:** not in CI, need the full app running, and rely on a Chrome password-popup workaround in `BaseE2ETest` | Medium | Low | Tagged `e2e` and skipped by CI so they cannot break the PR gate; how to run them is in the README; run `mvnw test -Pe2e` before each demo; remove the workaround (`TODO(e2e)`) when demo passwords change | Documentation Lead | 2026-10-11 | Mitigated |
| R8 | **Operability gaps:** no health endpoint, and API response time is not yet measured (NFR-6, NFR-8) | Medium | Medium | Add a health endpoint (e.g. Spring Boot Actuator) for the deploy smoke test; measure p95 from `CorrelationFilter`'s `durationMs` logs and record it in `nfrs.md` | Technical Lead | 2026-10-11 | Open |
